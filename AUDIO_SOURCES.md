# Audio sources

The current simulator does not ship third-party audio files. Its engine, wind, rain, runway, gear, flap, warning, touchdown, and crash sounds are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.

These sources are approved for later recorded-sound upgrades:

- [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), CC0 1.0
- [Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), CC0 1.0
- [Jet Turbine Noise by qubodup](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/205581/), CC0 1.0
- [wind.wav by Divinux](https://freesound.org/people/Divinux/sounds/254642/), CC0 1.0
- [Rain.wav by idomusics](https://freesound.org/people/idomusics/sounds/518863/), CC0 1.0
- [Collision by qubodup](https://freesound.org/s/332058/), CC0 1.0

Before bundling a recorded file, preserve its original filename, source URL, creator, displayed license, download date, and SHA-256 checksum here. Do not add audio marked CC BY, CC BY-NC, or "royalty free" without a redistribution review.
